When telemedicine works

The right care, without the commute.

Telemedicine isn't a substitute for every visit, but for many situations it's just as effective as an in-person appointment — and far more convenient. Dr. Sharma's virtual visits cover:

  • Chronic disease management — diabetes, hypertension, high cholesterol, thyroid disorders
  • Weight management — including evaluation for GLP-1 medications when appropriate
  • Prescription refills and medication adjustments
  • Follow-up visits after lab work, in-person exams, or hospital stays
  • Acute concerns — minor illness, viral symptoms, urinary infections, skin conditions
  • Mental health and stress consultation
  • Care coordination with specialists and review of outside records

Common questions

Telemedicine, answered.

Are you licensed in California?

Yes. Dr. Sharma is licensed to practice medicine in California and follows California Medical Board telehealth guidelines. Telemedicine visits are limited to patients physically located in California at the time of the appointment.

Are visits HIPAA-compliant?

Yes. We use a secure, HIPAA-compliant video platform for all telemedicine visits. Patient information, video sessions, and electronic records are all protected under federal HIPAA standards.

Are visits live or asynchronous?

All telemedicine consultations are synchronous — meaning you and Dr. Sharma are on a live video call together. Patient intake forms and history may be filled out in advance through our EMR system, which makes the live visit more efficient.

How are prescriptions handled?

Prescriptions are sent electronically through SureScripts to the pharmacy of your choice — based on whichever location is most convenient or has the best price. For controlled substances, we use ECPS-compliant electronic prescribing.

Does insurance cover telemedicine?

Most major insurance plans cover telemedicine at the same rate as in-person visits. We'll verify your coverage when you book. We also accept cash payment for patients without insurance — typical visits range from $125 to $250.

Do you partner with any specific pharmacy?

No. We have no partnerships with any pharmacy. You choose where your prescriptions are filled — we simply send the e-prescription wherever you prefer.

What if I need labs or imaging?

Lab orders are sent electronically to the location of your choice — typically Quest, LabCorp, or your hospital lab. Imaging orders work the same way. Results come back to us, and we follow up with you to discuss findings.

What's your cancellation and refund policy?

Telemedicine evaluations follow a no-refund policy once the consultation has taken place. If a payment is made but no evaluation occurs within 30 days, patients may request a 50% refund (excluding payment processing fees). Full details are available in our Terms of Service.
