1. Services We Provide

Atlas Intensive Care offers a range of medical services including, but not limited to:

  • USCIS-designated Civil Surgeon services, including I-693 immigration medical examinations, DS-1843 emigration exams, and N-648 medical disability waivers
  • Primary care and family medicine, including annual physicals and preventive screenings
  • Chronic disease management for conditions including diabetes, hypertension, hyperlipidemia, COPD, asthma, thyroid disorders, and obesity
  • Telemedicine consultations for eligible patients located in California
  • Concierge medicine and home visit services
  • Vaccinations, weight management, and preventive care
  • Pre-employment, school, and sports physicals

We provide all services directly. We do not subcontract medical evaluations to third parties. Specific services may evolve over time as we add capabilities or update our practice.

2. Telemedicine Services

What we offer

Our telemedicine offerings include:

  • Virtual consultations via a HIPAA-compliant video conferencing platform, covering management of conditions such as hypertension, diabetes, sexually transmitted diseases, urinary tract infections, viral illnesses, and obesity, among others
  • Remote monitoring for ongoing chronic disease management when wearable devices or other tracking tools are appropriate
  • E-prescriptions and digital health records through our integrated electronic medical record (EMR) system
  • Health and wellness webinars led by our healthcare professionals (registration handled through our website)
  • Telemedicine membership plans with tiered coverage levels for patients who prefer ongoing care subscriptions

Synchronous visits only

All telemedicine consultations are synchronous — you and Dr. Sharma are connected live via video. Patient intake forms may be completed asynchronously through our EMR system before your visit, but the consultation itself is always live.

Time to consultation

Most appointments are scheduled within 1–2 hours to 14 days from the time of your request, depending on urgency, time of year, and the requested appointment window.

3. Eligibility & Patient Verification

To receive telemedicine services, you must:

  • Be physically located in California at the time of your appointment
  • Be at least 18 years of age, or have a parent or legal guardian present if a minor
  • Provide a current government-issued photo ID (such as a state-issued ID or driver's license) for identity verification
  • Provide accurate medical history and current symptoms to the best of your knowledge

Before evaluation, we confirm your home address using a current State ID or driver's license. This verification is required by California telehealth regulations and helps ensure you are eligible to receive care under California Medical Board guidelines. Additional providers, when added in the future, will be appropriately licensed and disclosed on our website.

5. Refund Policy

Our refund policy is designed to be fair to both patients and our practice:

Completed evaluations

Once a medical evaluation has been completed (in person or via telemedicine), the consultation fee is non-refundable. The clinical work, time, and documentation involved cannot be returned.

Partial evaluations

If a payment has been made and a partial evaluation has been conducted, you may submit a refund request within 30 days. Approved partial-evaluation refunds are issued at 15% of the fees paid, excluding payment processing charges, credited back to the original payment method.

Unscheduled or unexecuted evaluations

If a payment has been made but no evaluation occurs within 30 days, you may request a refund of 50% of the fees paid, excluding payment processing charges, credited back to the original payment method.

How to request a refund

Submit refund requests in writing to info@atlasintensivecare.com with your name, date of service, and reason for the request. We aim to process eligible refunds within 10 business days of approval.

6. Prescriptions & Pharmacy

When prescriptions are appropriate at the conclusion of an evaluation, we use the integrated SureScripts e-prescribing platform to send prescriptions electronically to your pharmacy of choice. For controlled substances, we use Electronic Prescribing of Controlled Substances (EPCS) in compliance with DEA requirements.

We have no partnerships with any pharmacy. You are free to choose any licensed pharmacy based on location, price, or convenience. We do not receive any compensation, referral fees, or other consideration from pharmacies for sending prescriptions to them.

The decision to prescribe any medication is made solely by the treating physician based on clinical appropriateness. We reserve the right to decline to prescribe any medication when we determine it is not in the patient's best interest.
